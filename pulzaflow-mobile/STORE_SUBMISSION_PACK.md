Social, with participation.

## Store description
PULZA FLOW is a social platform built around participation. Share posts and Pulzas, invite people to participate, react, comment, vote, discover profiles and stay connected through notifications.

Create a normal post when you want to share something. Create a Pulza when you want people to participate.

Core features:
- Create posts and interactive Pulzas
- Add images and videos
- React and comment
- Vote on Pulza choices
- Discover people and profiles
- Follow and unfollow accounts
- Receive activity notifications
- Report content or accounts
- Block accounts
- Edit your profile and profile photo
- Delete your account from inside the app

## Monetization
PULZA FLOW is free to download and uses advertising as its monetization model. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in the Google Play version.
