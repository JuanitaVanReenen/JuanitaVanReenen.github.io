# PULZA FLOW — Community Guidelines

## Purpose

PULZA FLOW is a social platform built around participation. These guidelines apply to Posts, Pulzas, comments, profiles, photos, videos and other user content.

## Do not post

- Illegal content or content that facilitates illegal activity.
- Child sexual abuse or exploitation material.
- Credible threats of violence or encouragement of violence.
- Harassment, bullying, stalking or targeted abuse.
- Hate or attacks targeting protected characteristics.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery or sexual exploitation.
- Doxxing or malicious disclosure of private personal information.
- Fraud, impersonation or deceptive schemes.
- Spam, scams or coordinated abuse.
- Content that infringes another person's intellectual-property rights.
- Malicious software, credential theft or attempts to compromise accounts.
- Content whose primary purpose is to exploit, abuse or endanger minors.

## Participation rules

- Respect other participants.
- Do not manipulate votes or engagement through deception, bots or coordinated abuse.
- Do not use Pulzas to target or humiliate another person.
- Do not evade blocks or moderation restrictions through another account.

## Moderation

Users can report a Pulza or user and can block users from inside the app. Reports are reviewed through the private moderation process. Actions may include removal of content, restriction, suspension or dismissal of a report.

PULZA FLOW may remove content or restrict accounts when necessary to enforce these rules, protect users, comply with law, or protect the integrity of the service.

## Appeals / support

The final production policy must provide a support contact and an operational process for users to raise concerns about moderation decisions.

## Acceptance

Users must accept the final Terms of Use / Community Guidelines before they can create or upload user-generated content.

This document is a product-policy draft and must be reviewed for applicable law before publication.
