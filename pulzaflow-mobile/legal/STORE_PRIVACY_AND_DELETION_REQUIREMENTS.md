# PULZA FLOW — Privacy, account deletion and store-readiness requirements

## Privacy policy

The final policy must be publicly accessible and linked both:
- inside PULZA FLOW
- in the Apple App Store listing
- in Google Play Console

It must accurately describe the data actually used by the released version, including account information, user-generated content, media, authentication, notifications, subscription information and any analytics/SDK data actually added.

Do not publish a policy claiming use of analytics, advertising, location, contacts, microphone or other data unless the released app actually uses that data.

## Account deletion

PULZA FLOW must provide a clear in-app account deletion path.

The deletion flow must delete the account and associated user data, subject to clearly disclosed legal/security retention requirements.

Google Play additionally requires an external web resource for account-deletion requests when the app supports account creation.

Apple requires account deletion to be initiated inside the app for apps that support account creation.

## Subscription handling

Before deleting an account with an auto-renewing subscription, the app must clearly explain how subscription billing is handled. Deleting the app/account does not automatically mean the store subscription is cancelled.

## User-generated content

The deletion design must cover:
- profile
- posts/Pulzas
- comments
- reactions
- votes
- follows
- blocks
- notifications
- reports where legally/operationally appropriate
- uploaded media

Any retention exception must be documented in the final privacy policy.

## Store declarations

Before submission, prepare accurate:
- Apple privacy disclosures
- Google Play Data safety section
- privacy-policy URL
- external account-deletion URL
- developer contact information

These must match the actual production build and third-party SDK configuration.
