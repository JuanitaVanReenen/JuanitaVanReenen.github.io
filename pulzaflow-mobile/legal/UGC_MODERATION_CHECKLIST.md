# PULZA FLOW — UGC moderation release checklist

PULZA FLOW is a user-generated-content social app. Before store submission, the production build must include:

- Report objectionable Pulzas.
- Report users.
- Block users.
- Hide blocked users' content.
- A published Terms of Use / Community Guidelines.
- A moderation workflow for reviewing reports.
- A mechanism for timely response to valid abuse reports.
- Content filtering/moderation appropriate to the released feature set.
- A published support/contact method.
- Clear rules explaining prohibited content and conduct.
- Account deletion that covers the user's associated content/data, subject to disclosed lawful retention.

Apple's current App Review Guidelines for UGC require filtering, reporting, blocking and published contact information. Google Play's current UGC policy similarly requires robust ongoing moderation, reporting and blocking.

This checklist does not claim that the production moderation operation is live. A human moderation process and the required backend/admin tooling still need to be deployed and tested before release.
