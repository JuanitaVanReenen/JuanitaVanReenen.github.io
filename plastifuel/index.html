PLASTIFUEL™

Pre-pilot engineering concept for converting controlled PE/PP-rich plastic waste into a gasoline-range hydrocarbon blendstock candidate through feedstock gating, process control, early product analysis and integrated thermal/process architecture.

Development status: PLASTIFUEL is a pre-pilot engineering technology package. The visual is a technology concept illustration; it does not represent a commissioned commercial plant or validated fuel performance.

Controlled feedstock

PE/PP-rich waste is screened and routed through defined acceptance and contaminant-gating logic.

Quality-led processing

Early product analysis supports decisions on whether additional conditioning or upgrading is required.

Commercial objective

The engineering model focuses on qualified product cost per litre rather than gross liquid yield alone.
