# Sulia — Buyer Demo Script

## Purpose
A short acquisition-facing walkthrough of the working Sulia messaging MVP.

## Sequence
**0:00–0:05 — Home**
Show the Sulia chat home and establish the clean messaging experience.

**0:05–0:12 — Text**
Open a conversation and send a normal text message. This establishes that Sulia works as a familiar messenger before introducing its differentiator.

**0:12–0:22 — Face Message**
Open Face Message and show the in-app recording state. Explain that the recipient can receive a personal face-to-face message rather than only text.

**0:22–0:30 — Face Viewer**
Show the received Face Message view.

**0:30–0:38 — Face Reply**
Reply using Face Reply to demonstrate two-way face-based communication.

**0:38–0:45 — Face Reaction**
Add a Face Reaction to show a lightweight personal response.

**0:45–0:55 — Supporting product**
Briefly show Contacts, Notifications and Profile to demonstrate that Sulia is a broader messaging product, not a single-feature screen.

**0:55–1:00 — Close**
End on:
“Text when words are enough. Voice when you want to be heard. Face when you want to be there.”

## Recording rule
The final acquisition video should be recorded from the actual working Sulia interface. Do not present generated or illustrative footage as a recording of the product.
