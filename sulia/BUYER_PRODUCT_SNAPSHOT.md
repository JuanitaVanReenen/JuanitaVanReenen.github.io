# Sulia — Buyer Product Snapshot

## Product
Sulia

## One-line description
A standalone messaging experience designed to make personal communication feel more present by combining text, voice, Face Messages, Face Replies and Face Reactions.

## Core differentiator
Sulia is built around the idea that some messages are better when the person comes through. The product lets a conversation move naturally from text to voice to face-based communication.

## Current MVP
- Chat home
- Conversations
- Text messaging interaction
- Voice-message interaction
- Face Message interaction
- Face Message viewer
- Face Reply
- Face Reaction
- Face Introduction concept
- Contacts
- Notifications
- Profile

## Public demonstration
https://juanitavanreenen.github.io/sulia/

## Acquisition positioning
Sulia is being developed as a standalone product/IP opportunity for a strategic buyer. The objective is an outright acquisition/assignment rather than a SaaS operating business.

## Buyer-facing language
Do not describe Sulia as a WhatsApp replacement, a generic video messenger, or an AI product. Lead with the communication concept and the working MVP.
