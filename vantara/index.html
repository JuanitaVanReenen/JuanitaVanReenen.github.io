What VANTARA is

VANTARA is defined as a complete vehicle concept, not a styling-only mock-up. The engineering package covers the complete bakkie: body and chassis, powertrain, driveline, suspension, steering, brakes, airbags, crash structure, ADAS, Dynamic Surround Awareness, electrical/electronic systems, HVAC, lighting, infotainment, load bed, towing and validation.

Distinctive vehicle architecture

The approved VANTARA design combines a premium double-cab proportion, integrated load-bed identity and a purpose-designed vehicle system architecture.

Complete vehicle engineering

The package covers structure, propulsion, chassis, restraint systems, electronics, thermal systems, cabin systems, utility functions and manufacturing/validation.

New safety-awareness system

Dynamic Surround Awareness is proposed as a spatial vehicle-awareness architecture rather than an ordinary stitched 360° camera view.

Vehicle character

The visual direction is deliberately premium, compact-looking and purposeful rather than oversized or exaggerated.

The complete vehicle package

The vehicle definition includes the equipment and systems expected around the adaptive architecture.

  • Body and passenger safety structure
  • Steering system and controls
  • Braking system and brake routing
  • Powertrain and driveline architecture
  • Hybrid/electric energy architecture provision
  • Thermal management and cooling
  • LV/HV electrical distribution and wiring
  • Vehicle networking and diagnostics
  • Forward, rear and side cameras
  • 360-degree surround-view provision
  • Parking and proximity sensing
  • ADAS sensor provisions
  • Front/rear 360-degree safety beacon architecture
  • Exterior and interior lighting
  • Infotainment and digital instrument display
  • Sound system, microphones and speakers
  • HVAC, filtration and demist/defrost
  • Glazing, mirrors, wipers and washers
  • Seats, belts, pretensioners and restraint provisions
  • Airbag and occupant-protection integration
  • Doors, locks, windows and tailgate/access
  • Driver HMI and adaptive-state display
  • Emergency and recovery equipment
  • Cargo area, tie-downs and practical equipment
  • Vehicle-level sensors and fault memory
  • Cybersecurity and software-control provisions
  • Service isolation and maintenance access
  • Manufacturing, tolerance and validation requirements
The 360° / horizontal safety architecture is part of the vehicle.

Front and rear integrated safety modules are designed into the vehicle architecture, with directional visibility toward forward, rearward and lateral/oblique viewing zones. Final lighting performance must still be engineered and tested against the target-market regulations.

Engineering status

The VANTARA package is complete at preliminary engineering and acquisition-package level. The next real-world stage is detailed CAD/CAE, supplier-grade component definition, prototype fabrication, physical testing and professional IP/legal review.
