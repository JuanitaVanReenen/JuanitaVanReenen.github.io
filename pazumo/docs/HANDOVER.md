## Buyer productionisation
A buyer can take the source forward into its own infrastructure. Production work is explicitly separated from the transferable core so that third-party credentials, accounts and infrastructure choices are not embedded in the asset.

## Transfer principle
The asset is designed for outright software/IP acquisition and handover rather than requiring the original developer to operate a SaaS business.
