MoneyQuest Kids

Interactive financial and life-skills learning software for young people. MoneyQuest Kids turns realistic money and life decisions into guided learning journeys.

The software has been developed as a standalone product that can be adopted, branded, expanded and implemented by a bank, education organisation or other strategic partner. The current demonstration shows the core product experience; production banking integration would be completed within the implementing organisation's systems and requirements.

Bank implementation

Designed to be implemented by a bank

MoneyQuest Kids can be adapted into a bank-branded youth financial-learning and engagement experience. A bank could customise the branding, learning content, local currency, age journeys, savings-related challenges and other educational experiences.

Potential implementation model: the bank's existing youth-banking environment remains the banking system of record, while MoneyQuest Kids provides the interactive learning layer around financial decisions and goals. Subject to the bank's architecture, security, privacy and compliance requirements, the software can be connected to appropriate digital channels and systems.

Current product boundary

Clear and transparent

MoneyQuest Kids is a developed software demonstration/MVP. It does not claim to be a live banking system, does not process real banking transactions in this demonstration, and does not claim an existing integration with any bank. A production implementation would be completed with the adopting organisation's technology, security, privacy, child-safety and compliance teams.
