Autonomous Modular Work Vehicle

A rugged four-wheel industrial work vehicle concept built around configurable work modules, autonomous or supervised operation and an integrated work architecture for demanding outdoor environments.

Concept status: This is a physical industrial product concept. Potential IP focus is the integrated architecture, implementation and relationships between the vehicle, sensing, work interfaces, modules and operating configurations. Prior-art and patentability work remains required.

Core architecture

The concept brings together power, compute/control, sensing, work interface, load/tool modules and safety within one configurable vehicle architecture.

  • Travel — compact and protected configuration
  • Carry — cargo and tool configuration
  • Work — work module deployed
  • Inspect — elevated sensing configuration

Potential applications

Construction sites, infrastructure maintenance, industrial yards, mining and quarry support, inspection and surveying, utility work and other difficult outdoor environments.

Possible module directions include excavation, grading, lifting/material handling, utility carrying, drilling, inspection and vegetation/brush management, subject to engineering validation.

Development direction

Future engineering work would address structure, suspension, steering, braking, drive, energy storage, thermal management, hydraulics/electrics, tool interfaces, payload and stability, autonomous navigation, communications, emergency systems, environmental sealing and compliance.

Commercial discussion

The concept can be discussed with organisations interested in further development, strategic integration or potential outright acquisition, subject to due diligence and agreement on the defined assets and rights.
