# Capital Decision Analyzer — Buyer Brief

## Product
Capital Decision Analyzer is a focused digital investment decision-support product.

## Core proposition
**See the numbers before you commit the capital.**

## What it does
The tool lets a user enter an investment structure and evaluate:
- NPV
- IRR
- MIRR
- ROI
- Payback
- Discounted payback
- Profitability Index
- Cash-flow projections
- Scenario variations
- Sensitivity to key assumptions
- Break-even context
- Side-by-side comparison of up to three investments
- A printable decision report

## Product model
Standalone browser-based software. The initial demonstrator performs calculations locally in the browser and does not require a user account.

## Intended strategic value
Potentially suitable as a component, white-label tool, educational finance product, business-case tool, or acquisition target for companies operating in financial software, planning, analytics, corporate finance, investment analysis, or business decision-support.

## Boundary
This is decision-support software, not personalized financial advice, investment solicitation, or a guarantee of future investment performance.
