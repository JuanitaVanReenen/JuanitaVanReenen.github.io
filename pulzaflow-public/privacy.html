PULZA FLOW Privacy Policy

Version 1.0

PULZA FLOW uses information needed to provide the service. This includes account email, profile information, user-generated posts/Pulzas, comments, reactions, votes, follows, blocks, reports, uploaded media, notifications and subscription/entitlement information.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide social features, store and display content, deliver notifications, enforce safety rules, process subscriptions and respond to support requests.

User-generated content

Content you submit may be visible to other users according to the product's social features. Reports and moderation records may be retained when necessary for safety, abuse prevention or legal obligations.

Media

Images and videos you upload are stored to provide media features. Access controls and signed URLs are used for private stored media where applicable.

Subscriptions

If you purchase PULZA FLOW PLUS, purchase and entitlement information is processed through the relevant app store and subscription service. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an app-store subscription.

Account deletion

You can initiate account deletion inside the app. See Account deletion.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the released product's data practices materially change.
