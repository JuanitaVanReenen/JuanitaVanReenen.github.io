PULZA FLOW Account Deletion

In-app deletion: Sign in to PULZA FLOW → Profile → Delete account.

What deletion covers

The deletion process is designed to remove the account and associated user data, including profile and social content metadata and uploaded media, subject to limited retention required for security, abuse prevention or legal obligations.

Subscription warning

Deleting your PULZA FLOW account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel the store subscription separately.

If you cannot access the app

Use the support route published with the current store listing to request assistance with account deletion. Do not send your password or payment credentials.
