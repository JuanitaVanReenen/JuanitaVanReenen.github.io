PULZA FLOW Community Guidelines

PULZA FLOW is built for participation. Keep participation safe, lawful and respectful.

Do not post

  • Credible threats or encouragement of violence.
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse.
  • Content involving exploitation of minors.
  • Targeted harassment, intimidation or malicious impersonation.
  • Fraud, illegal activity or instructions intended to facilitate serious wrongdoing.
  • Spam or abusive automated activity.

Reporting and blocking

Users can report Pulzas and block accounts inside the app. Reports are reviewed through the service's moderation process and appropriate action may include removal, restriction or suspension.
