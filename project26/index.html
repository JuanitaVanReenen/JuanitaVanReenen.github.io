Intelligence insight

If internet connectivity is lost, locally connected devices may continue operating, while cloud-dependent services and remote access can be affected. Kaylics makes those relationships visible before the homeowner has to discover them during an incident.

Product architecture

MAP → TRACE → SIMULATE

Kaylics is deliberately not another smart-home control platform. The working MVP demonstrates an intelligence and visualization layer that could sit above existing smart-home, appliance, telecom, energy or security ecosystems.

Map

Represent rooms, devices and home systems as a connected model so relationships are understandable at a glance.

Trace

Follow dependencies from a network, device or system change and identify what may be affected downstream.

Simulate

Explore “What Happens If?” scenarios such as internet loss, power failure, router changes or adding a new device.

Strategic positioning: Kaylics is a demonstration MVP. A strategic buyer could replace the sample home model with its own devices, rules, integrations, branding and customer experience. The current MVP makes no claim of live device control or real-time telemetry.
