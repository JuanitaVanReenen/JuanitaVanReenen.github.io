Where can reconciliation software be useful?

Data reconciliation can be useful when an organization moves information between systems or datasets and needs an independent way to investigate whether records changed during the transfer.

  • ERP and business-system migrations
  • System-to-system data transfers
  • Construction and operational software migrations
  • Migration validation and data-quality checks
  • Pre-production or post-migration reconciliation workflows

Frequently asked questions

Is BuildCheck a production integration?

No. It is a working browser-based software MVP. It is not represented as a live production integration with a third-party platform.

What does the demonstration use?

The demonstration uses controlled synthetic data. It is not customer data and does not represent customer results.
