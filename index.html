Strategic model

Build → Validate → Acquire → Hand Over

The portfolio is developed around a simple transfer model: create a focused technology asset, validate the solution, identify an organisation that can use it strategically, and transfer the asset for further development or integration.

01 / BUILD

Focused solution

Develop a defined technology solution around a specific business problem.

02 / VALIDATE

Demonstrable asset

Turn the solution into an understandable, demonstrable product or technology package.

03 / ACQUIRE

Strategic buyer

Present the relevant asset to organisations where the technology has a potential strategic fit.

04 / HAND OVER

Transfer

Agree the transaction and provide the defined materials for further development, integration or ownership.
