Frequently asked questions

Are these physical products or digital downloads?

These are digital products. No physical planner, diary or book will be posted. The individual Gumroad listing explains the files included and their formats.

Can I print the planners and diaries?

Products described as printable PDFs can be printed at home or through a print service. Check the product page for the stated page size and file format before purchase.

Can I use the money planner in my own currency?

The money planners are designed to be adapted to your own currency. The templates help organise information and are not financial, tax or investment advice.

What file formats are included?

Depending on the product, downloads may include PDF, Markdown or CSV files. The product page lists the included formats. CSV trackers can be opened in compatible spreadsheet software such as Excel or Google Sheets.
