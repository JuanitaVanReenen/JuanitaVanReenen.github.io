Strategic Development / Acquisition Opportunity

HEMAVIO

A compact reusable reader paired with a disposable multi-zone microfluidic cartridge, engineered around one capillary sample and an expandable assay architecture.

GlucoseHemoglobinTotal cholesterolExpandable cartridge

What it is

An integrated reader-and-cartridge architecture. The disposable component is a controlled microfluidic cartridge with multiple isolated assay/sensing zones rather than three ordinary consumer test strips.

Initial targets

Glucose
Hemoglobin
Total cholesterol

Additional analytes remain separate development targets until their own assay and verification evidence exists.

How the system is intended to operate

INSERT → IDENTIFY → VERIFY CARTRIDGE → SAMPLE READY → SAMPLE VALIDATION → RUN → DETECT → VALIDATE → RESULT / INVALID

The design uses a sample-quality gate, defined-volume metering, controlled distribution, physical isolation between assay zones and fail-closed result logic. Invalid conditions are intended to block numerical-result display.

Strategic opportunity

The controlled engineering package is available for a qualified company or development partner to take into physical prototyping, assay development, analytical verification, regulatory development and commercialization.

Buyer disclosure

This is a development-stage engineering platform. Measurement targets are not medically validated. Accuracy, clinical performance, manufacturing qualification and regulatory status have not been established.

Designed ≠ built.
Built ≠ verified.
Verified ≠ medically validated.
Medically validated ≠ regulatory approval.
