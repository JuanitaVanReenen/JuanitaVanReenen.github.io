Refund Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy describes how refund and cancellation requests for MoneyMismatch subscriptions are handled. Any non-waivable rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

1. Subscription charges

Subscription prices and billing intervals are displayed before checkout. Unless stated otherwise at checkout or required by law, subscription charges cover the billing period purchased and are not automatically refundable merely because the service was unused or only partly used.

2. Requesting a refund

If you believe you were charged in error, were charged more than once, or have another reason for requesting a refund, contact us at vanreenen.juanita@yahoo.com with the account email, transaction date, and a description of the issue. Do not send full card numbers or security codes by email. Payment processing and any refund may be handled by Paddle under its applicable procedures.

3. Cancellation and renewal

You may request help with cancelling a subscription by contacting us at the email above. Cancellation stops future renewals when processed, but does not automatically refund a charge already made. Access may remain available until the end of the paid period, subject to the plan and payment-provider terms.

4. Duplicate or incorrect charges

Please contact us promptly if you believe a charge is duplicate or incorrect. We will review the information and coordinate with the payment provider where appropriate.

5. Legal rights and updates

Nothing in this policy limits refund or cancellation rights that apply under mandatory law or the payment provider's applicable terms. We may update this policy by publishing a revised version here.
