Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how MoneyMismatch may process information when you visit or use the service.

1. Information you provide

If you create an account, we process the email address and authentication information needed to manage sign-in. If you use workspace features, we may process workspace names, comparison summaries, exception details, and amounts associated with a saved comparison.

2. Files and financial records

CSV files selected for comparison are read by the application in your browser. If you are signed in and have a workspace, the application may save comparison summaries and generated exception records to its database. Do not upload personal or confidential financial information unless you are authorized to do so and have assessed whether the service is appropriate for that information.

3. Service providers

The service uses Supabase for authentication and database functionality, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Paddle for subscription checkout and payment processing. These providers may process information as needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy documentation.

4. Payments

Payment details submitted during checkout are handled by Paddle and are not intentionally stored in MoneyMismatch's application database. Refer to Paddle's privacy information for details about payment processing.

5. Purposes and sharing

Information is used to operate and secure the service, provide account and workspace functionality, handle subscriptions, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, subject to legal requirements and operational needs. We use third-party infrastructure and reasonable measures, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You can contact us to ask about personal information associated with your use of the service or request account-related assistance. Applicable privacy laws may give you additional rights depending on your location.

8. Children and changes

MoneyMismatch is intended for business use and is not designed for children. We may update this policy by publishing a revised version on this page.
