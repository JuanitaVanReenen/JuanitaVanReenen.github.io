Automotive design concept

KAVORA

An original 4x4 bakkie concept developed as a distinctive visual and product direction: rugged, premium and designed around practical utility without presenting unverified production specifications.

Exterior direction

Muscular double-cab proportions, angular lighting, protective off-road hardware, wide stance and a premium expedition-oriented finish.

Utility architecture

A concept direction for integrating cargo organisation, attachment points, power/data interfaces and work-oriented functionality around the bed.

Development opportunity

The visual concept can be developed further by an automotive, mobility, manufacturing or strategic design organisation.

Concept status: KAVORA is presented as an original design/product concept. Specific novelty, patentability, engineering specifications and production feasibility require separate professional validation.
