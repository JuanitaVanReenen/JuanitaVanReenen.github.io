# Evidence Quality Specification

## Purpose
Distinguish the presence of evidence from the quality and verification strength of that evidence.

## Quality dimensions
- source identified
- exact locator identified
- verification status
- integrity verification where applicable
- provenance completeness

## Principle
Evidence presence alone must not imply that a requirement is established.

## Initial quality states
Strong, Adequate, Weak, Insufficient.

## Rules
- Missing source or locator is insufficient for traceable evidence.
- Verified evidence can be adequate even where cryptographic integrity is not available.
- Strong evidence requires verified status plus integrity verification in this foundation.
- Quality assessment does not replace domain-expert judgment.

## Production direction
Organizations should configure evidence-quality thresholds by requirement type, domain and gate, with authorized reviewers able to document justified exceptions.
