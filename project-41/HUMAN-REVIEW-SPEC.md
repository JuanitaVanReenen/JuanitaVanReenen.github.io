# Human Review Specification

## Purpose
Provide a structured record for qualified human review of automated assurance outputs.

## Review dispositions
- confirmed
- challenged
- deferred

## Required record
Reviewer identity, subject under review, timestamp, disposition, observations, evidence references and rationale.

## Principles
- Automated findings remain preserved when challenged.
- A reviewer can defer a conclusion while creating follow-up actions.
- Human review is distinct from authorization to record a final gate decision.
- Human review cannot silently modify source evidence.

## Production direction
Review permissions, reviewer qualifications, conflicts of interest, review evidence and audit history should be enforced through authenticated governance controls.
