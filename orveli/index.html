The core mechanic

Simple enough to understand in seconds.

A person completes a digital experience once. Orveli turns the important states and actions into a replayable experience. A second person can follow it and take control at a selected point.

01 / COMPLETE

Someone does it

A user completes a process inside a digital product.

02 / CAPTURE

Orveli maps it

The important actions and states become a replayable sequence.

03 / REPLAY

Someone follows it

Another user can experience the process step by step.

04 / TAKE OVER

They make a choice

The viewer takes control and explores an alternative path.

What makes it different

Not a screen recording.

A recording shows what happened. Orveli is designed around the underlying experience so the person replaying it can interact with the process.
