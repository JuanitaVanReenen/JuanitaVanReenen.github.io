Design language

One vehicle. One visual identity.

VEYORA is defined by a clean aerodynamic nose, narrow lighting, a floating dark glasshouse, strong shoulder surfacing, geometric wheels, flush details and a distinctive illuminated rear signature.

Strong shoulder / floating roof

Long, clean surfacing and a dark glasshouse create the core side identity.

Rear Safety Spine

The horizontal illuminated rear feature is integrated into the vehicle design language.

Low, wide proportions

Concept dimensions are placeholders for design development, not engineering specifications.

Minimal premium cabin

Wide display architecture, clean surfaces and a restrained premium interior direction.

Safety Spine

Integrated rear communication concept.

The Safety Spine is a design feature across the rear that can be developed as a multifunction illuminated signal. Its final mechanism, lighting behaviour and regulatory implementation remain development work.
