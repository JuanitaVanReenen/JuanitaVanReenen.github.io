Original automotive design concept

VEYORA —
designed to be built.

A complete vehicle design direction for a futuristic premium crossover/SUV, developed as an original exterior and interior concept for an automotive company to evaluate, engineer and potentially take forward into production.

Concept status: visual and design concept. Dimensions, mechanisms, engineering feasibility, manufacturing and regulatory compliance require professional validation by a development partner.

Design language

One vehicle. One visual identity.

VEYORA is defined by a clean aerodynamic nose, narrow lighting, a floating dark glasshouse, strong shoulder surfacing, geometric wheels, flush details and a distinctive illuminated rear signature.

Exterior identity

Strong shoulder surfacing, floating roof and a clean aerodynamic profile.

Rear Safety Spine

A horizontal illuminated rear feature integrated into the vehicle design language.

Interior direction

Minimal premium cabin, wide display architecture and clean material direction.

Development direction

Concept proportions and mechanisms remain subject to engineering and regulatory validation.

Safety Spine

Integrated rear communication concept.

The Safety Spine is a design feature across the rear that can be developed as a multifunction illuminated signal. Its final mechanism, lighting behaviour and regulatory implementation remain development work.

Strategic opportunity

A design package an automotive company can take forward.
